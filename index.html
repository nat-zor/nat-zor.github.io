Natalia L. Zorrilla Sirlin


Welcome!



I’m Natalia Zorrilla Sirlin, an Assistant Researcher at CONICET (Argentina’s National Scientific and Technical Research Council), based at the Instituto de Filosofía Ezequiel de Olaso.


I am a former MSC Postdoctoral Fellow at McGill University and Ca' Foscari University of Venice, PI of "GYNODICY: gender-egalitarian fictions of origin in European philosophical culture (1673-1751)".
