Natalia L. Zorrilla Sirlin


Welcome!



I’m Natalia Zorrilla Sirlin, MSC Postdoctoral Fellow at McGill University and Ca' Foscari University of Venice and Assistant Researcher in CONICET (currently on leave).


My research project is titled "GYNODICY: gender-egalitarian fictions of origin in European philosophical culture (1673-1751)".
